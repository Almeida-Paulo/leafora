How to support a project

  1. Get to know the project

    The project page explains what will be restored and how the team plans to do it. This helps you understand the initiative before deciding to take part.

  2. Choose your contribution

    Choose an amount in USDC and confirm the contribution in your digital wallet on the Stellar network. You can contribute again while funding remains open.

  3. Follow project progress

    Return to the project page to read updates from the team. Your portfolio brings together the contributions recorded for your wallet.

Project types

  • REDD+

    Conserving existing forests and reducing deforestation and forest degradation.

  • ARR

    Restoring vegetation cover through afforestation, reforestation and revegetation.

  • ALM

    Farming practices that improve soil, water and vegetation management, such as cover crops and reduced tillage.

These are internationally recognized areas of practice. Methodology and validation depend on each project.

How Leafora works

Restoring a spring or a degraded area takes funding and ongoing care. Leafora is being built to connect you with the people doing that work, making small contributions possible and showing how each project progresses.

From contributing to following project progress.

Find a cause to support

Browse the catalog and explore each initiative, from native forest restoration to regenerative agriculture. Review its goals, stages and planned use of funds before taking part.

Choose your contribution

Choose an amount in USDC and confirm in your digital wallet. On the Stellar network, the contract records your contribution and allocation points in the same transaction.

Understand your contribution record

Each USDC corresponds to one allocation point (AP) in your chosen project. Points are recorded on Stellar and displayed in your portfolio. They are not carbon credits and do not guarantee financial returns.

See what happens next

Each project page brings together the team's updates and evidence. Your portfolio shows contributions associated with your wallet, even when you switch devices.

How you will be able to follow work in the field

A photo helps show the work, but we also need to know where and when it was taken. Leafora Capture will let teams record images together with that information at the moment of capture. You will be able to view published records on the project page.

Photos and videos will be stored outside the blockchain. A digital fingerprint recorded on the Sui network will make it possible to check whether a file has changed. Team review and independent verification will still be needed to assess what it shows.

Roadmap

The journey starts with USDC contributions on Stellar and progresses towards field records on Sui, environmental labels and a carbon credit marketplace. Explore what is planned for each stage and how projects will be monitored.

01Stellar Testnet

Choose a project and take part

You do not have to fund an entire project to help restore nature. Leafora aims to bring small contributions together and connect you with the people doing that work.

Before taking part, you can learn what the project aims to restore and how it plans to use the funds. The Leafora team selects which initiatives can be published.

For a project open for funding, choose an amount in USDC and confirm the contribution on the Stellar network through your wallet. The goal guides funding; contributions may exceed it until the deadline.

Each USDC contributed equals one allocation point (AP) in that project. Your portfolio shows recorded contributions and the progress of the initiatives you choose.

AP are recorded on Stellar by project and wallet. Participation NFTs are planned for a future stage on Sui; current points do not promise financial returns.

02Planned app

Leafora Capture

You will be able to follow the work through images that show where and when each activity was recorded.

With Leafora Capture, authorized team members will be able to document a project on their phones. Photos and videos will be linked to the activity being followed, helping you see how it changes over time.

Origin
Each record will identify the project and the person who captured it.
Context
The app will keep the capture location and time. You will also be able to see when the record's digital fingerprint was added to the Sui network.
Integrity
Images will be stored outside the blockchain. A digital fingerprint recorded on Sui will make it possible to check for changes.
Continuity
Without internet access, the team will be able to save records on the phone and send them once the connection returns, without creating duplicates.

The app will be released for Android first. Records will also be reviewed to assess whether they match the work carried out.

03Planned

Understand how projects will be assessed

To decide where to contribute, you need to understand both the proposal and what has been done. We want to make that assessment accessible and welcome more projects.

More projects, with oversight

Farmers, cooperatives and organizations will be able to submit their initiatives. The Leafora team will assess each proposal before authorizing publication and fundraising.

Following progress over time

Comparing field photos with satellite images will help track changes in an area. Artificial intelligence may flag differences for a person to review.

Independent verification

Independent specialists will assess what has been substantiated and what still needs clarification. You will be able to consult each project's reports and reviews.

04Planned

Show which project your company supports

Leafora labels will let companies show their support for nature through a clear connection to operating, audited projects.

We plan to use Sui to link each label to the project, company and evidence references. By checking a label, a customer will be able to review the contribution and the environmental claim that was verified.

A QR code will open the label's page, showing its supporting evidence, validity period and who verified it.

The page will also show whether the label has been renewed, suspended or revoked, keeping its history available.

The label will identify a substantiated contribution. By itself, it will not mean the company has offset its emissions or become carbon neutral.

05Planned

From environmental results to carbon credits

Some projects may generate credits by demonstrating that they reduced or removed emissions. We want to help them gather the data and undergo the necessary assessments.

A planting photo alone does not generate a carbon credit. A project must demonstrate its results and meet a certification program's requirements.

The assessment considers what would happen without the project and whether its benefits can last. Field records help document this work.

If credits are issued, you will be able to see which project they came from and how they were verified. Their identifiers will be checked against the issuing registry to help prevent duplicates.

How results will be assessed

We are studying references such as the GHG Protocol for emissions accounting and reporting, and certification programs such as Verra VCS and Gold Standard. The choice will depend on each project. Leafora does not currently claim certification or a partnership with these organizations.

06Future vision

Leafora Market

People looking to buy carbon credits will be able to check their origin and compare offers in one place. Those who issued them will be able to find buyers.

Leafora Market will bring together carbon credits and other environmental assets that meet its criteria. Before trading, you will be able to review the originating project, verification of its results and the asset's history.

We are also exploring trading participation rights in projects. Each offer will need to explain the buyer's rights and meet the applicable requirements.

Compare before buying

You will be able to compare offers and review each asset's information before choosing to buy.

Follow the transfer

After a trade, you will be able to follow the transfer and check the record of who owns the asset.

Record the use of a credit

When a credit is used to offset emissions, its retirement will be recorded so it cannot be used again.

This marketplace is still planned. Profit-sharing offers will depend on applicable rules, including Brazil's CVM requirements where relevant. Current points and planned NFTs do not grant these rights.

Questions about Leafora

How do contributions work today?

You can explore published projects and confirm USDC contributions through your wallet for enabled initiatives. The integration uses Stellar Testnet: tokens have no real financial value. Once the transaction is confirmed, points appear in your portfolio, grouped by project.

Is the app available yet?

The app is not yet publicly available. The screens show our planned field-recording experience. Access will start with authorized project teams, initially on Android.

Are allocation points carbon credits?

No. AP record your contribution on Stellar. Labels and NFTs planned on Sui will have separate purposes and will not, by themselves, be carbon credits. Credits require measured and verified results; supporting an initiative does not offset emissions.

When will the next stages launch?

We have not confirmed launch dates yet. We will share updates as each stage is ready, with the necessary technical validation, resources and partnerships.
