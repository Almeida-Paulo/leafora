How Leafora works

A platform to discover and support ecological projects: restoring springs and native forests, regenerative agriculture, rehabilitating degraded pastures and conserving biodiversity. Each project brings its funding goal, delivery plan and evidence together on one page.

Current stage · testing

What you can do at this stage.

Compare initiatives

Find projects by location, biome or impact. Review their purpose, funding goal, planned milestones and risks. The catalog identifies demonstration data.

Test wallet contributions

For enabled projects, choose a contribution tier and review the transaction in your wallet before signing. We currently use Sui devnet: these are test funds, not an investment with real money.

Understand your participation

The contract provides for supporter NFTs and Allocation Points, which record participation. Issuance requires an actual transaction. They are not carbon credits and do not guarantee profit or financial rights at this stage.

Review milestones and records

Each project page brings together published evidence and milestone progress. Your portfolio shows contributions signed in this browser by the connected wallet; it is not a complete blockchain history.

Planned app · Leafora Capture

How we plan to document work in the field.

Leafora Capture is a planned app for authorized operators to record photos, videos, geographic location, GPS accuracy and capture time. Each record will be linked to a project and milestone, with a signature and a hash: a digital fingerprint used to detect changes.

Media will be stored off-chain, with access and retention controls. Blockchain records will help verify integrity, but cannot alone prove that a photo, coordinate or claim is true. Field assessment, human review and audits remain essential.

Leafora Capture

An app to record photos, videos, location and time in the field.
Evidence linked to its project from the moment of capture.

Curators will grant access per project. Operators will select a milestone and capture media within the app, including metadata and a device signature. Android comes first, with iOS at a later stage.

Origin

Coordinates, GPS accuracy and capture date and time. The blockchain anchoring time is recorded separately.

Protected offline queue, synchronization and duplicate-record controls.

Before moving forward: field-test permissions, offline capture, timestamps, missing coordinates and duplicates. Signatures and hashes do not replace human review or independent verification.

04
Planned

Environmental labels linked to audited projects

Enable companies to communicate a specific environmental contribution, with an identifiable project, period, evidence and verification.

Environmental labels for companies, supported by documentary evidence from operating, audited projects. Each label should identify the contribution, period and result behind the claim.

  • A digital identifier or NFT linked to the project and company.
  • A public evidence page accessible through a QR code.
  • Scope, evidence, verifier and validity period.
  • Renewal, suspension and revocation with a retained history.

Before moving forward: define criteria, auditing, validity and usage rules. Supporting a project does not itself offset emissions or make a company carbon neutral.

05
Planned

Preparation for certified carbon credits

Organize measurement, reporting and verification so eligible projects can pursue certification and credit issuance.

Field photos and records support documentation but do not automatically generate credits. Issuance requires an applicable methodology, measurement, verified results and compliance with the chosen program's rules.

  • Baseline, additionality, permanence and the risk of emissions leakage.
  • Traceable origin, methodology, period, batch and serial numbers.
  • Reconciliation with the issuing registry to prevent double counting.

06
Planned marketplace

Leafora Market

Buy, sell and track eligible environmental assets, with each credit's origin, ownership and status identified.

Leafora Market is intended to bring together carbon credits and other eligible environmental assets. Buyers will be able to compare methodology, origin, period and history before trading.

Trading will only be enabled after technical validation, registry integration and legal requirements are established. Project participation rights depend on each offering's structure; current NFTs do not grant those rights.

Frequently asked questions

Understand the stages
and their limits.

What can I try today?

The project catalog, project pages and wallet connection on Sui devnet. Signed contributions require each project to be enabled. Test-network funds have no real financial value.

Is the app available yet?

Not yet. The screens on this page are concept previews. Leafora Capture is planned for Android first, with curators granting access to each project's operators.

Is a supporter NFT a carbon credit?

No. Supporter NFTs, Allocation Points, environmental labels and certified credits serve different purposes and have different requirements. A contribution does not guarantee profit, emissions offsets or future credits.

When will the next stages launch?

No dates are confirmed. Each stage depends on testing, pilots, resources, partnerships and, where needed, audits and approvals. The roadmap will be updated as these milestones are reached.
